A bundle is a three-pack of one of your own products, so its cost is three units landed and "three singles" is what a customer would pay buying them one at a time. The pack price matters. The saving is the reason to buy a pack: with none, only a few convenience buyers take it; priced above three singles, nobody does — the singles are on the same shelf.
