Research

There are twenty studies you can commission, priced from PKR 20,000 to PKR 200,000. Each answers one question — what the category is about to do, what rivals charge, why parcels come back, what your marketing really returned as against what the platforms claim it did. Some carry an error band, some arrive a month late, and re-buying one returns the same number, so there is nothing to average out.

Research buys information, never advantage. No study raises a number, wins a customer or moves your share. It only tells you whether the decision you are about to take is the right one — everything that actually moves is still your call. A team can win this game having bought nothing, and a team can lose it holding the whole catalogue.
