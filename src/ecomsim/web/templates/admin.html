What teams see in their monthly report

Decide each month which detail the teams' report shows. Hide it early in the game to make teams read the month from their totals and their own judgement, then release it. A month's column applies to that month's report, the decision form after it (stock and sales beside each product) and the campaigns page; you can change a month already run, and its report changes with it. Rankings, once published for a month, let teams see every month's ranking up to it, cumulative and month by month. The console always shows everything.
