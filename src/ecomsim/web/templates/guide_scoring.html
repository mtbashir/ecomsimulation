How you are scored

100 points across six pillars. Every measure is marked against fixed, published targets — not against the other teams — so every team in the room can score well. Market share is the only measure that depends on your rivals.

1 · Where you see it

Your monthly report

From month 2, the report ends with Scorecard, as it stands: your points on each pillar against its weight. Only your own score is shown.

The leaderboard

Withheld until month 4, so every team settles on a strategy before comparing itself with rivals. The final scorecard is published after month 12.

2 · How a measure is marked

Every measure has three published anchors: the value that scores nothing, the value that scores half, and the value that scores full marks. In between, marks rise in a straight line; beyond the ends they stop. A measure worth 8 points at its half-way anchor earns 4.

Example. Gross margin is worth 5 points: 30% scores nothing, 37% half, 44% full. A gross margin of 40.5% is three quarters of the way, so it earns 3.75 points.

3 · Which months count

Most measures are taken across the whole run, and later months count more: each month's weight is its number out of 78, so month 12 carries about 15% and month 1 about 1%. A weak start can be recovered; a strong start thrown away cannot.

Margins are the margins of the whole period — total profit over total revenue — not an average of monthly percentages, so a fat margin on a tiny month cannot lift the score. Customers, NPS, runway and cash are read as they stand at the end.

5 · What the scorecard punishes

Buying growth

Deep discounts and heavy ads raise revenue and orders, then cost you on margins, customer value and cash. Growth is 20 points; the pillars it can damage are 70.

Harvesting at the end

Cutting marketing in the last months lifts the margin for a moment, but the final months carry the most weight, revenue falls against your first month, and the customer base shrinks.

Hoarding cash

Runway is a band, not “most wins”: more than 9 months of idle cash scores less than 3 to 9 months. Running out scores nothing on the whole cash pillar.

Ignoring the memo

Decision quality is your instructor's judgement of your board memos: what you decided, why, and the number that will prove you right.
