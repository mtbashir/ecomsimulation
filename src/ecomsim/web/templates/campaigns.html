Who your ad budgets reach

Your Meta, TikTok and Google budgets are set on the decision form. This page decides how each one is spent: which products a campaign promotes and who sees it. The same rupees buy more visits that order when they reach the people who buy what you advertise.

Broad is the baseline. A channel with no campaigns runs one broad campaign — every product, everyone, everywhere — and that is exactly how your budgets have worked so far. Aim well and each rupee works harder than broad; aim at the wrong people and it works less hard. Each month's report shows every campaign's numbers and says why it did or did not work. Campaigns keep running until you change them. Read the marketing guide for what every number means and how to run a test.
